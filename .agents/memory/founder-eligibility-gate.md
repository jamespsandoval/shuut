---
name: Founder eligibility gate
description: Durable product rules for regional onboarding access and waitlisting.
---

Current access is for self-declared founders of technology startups based in Europe, including the UK. Existing onboarded founders are grandfathered. A new applicant who answers No is placed on the general-availability waitlist and cannot revise the answer.

**Why:** The initial community is intentionally limited by founder role, industry, and region, while preserving interest from people outside the launch audience.

**How to apply:** Keep the eligibility decision after name and editable generated username. Selection submits immediately without a separate confirmation button. Only an administrative or future general-availability process may change a waitlisted status.
