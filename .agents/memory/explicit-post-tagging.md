---
name: Explicit post tagging
description: Product rule for tagging people in SHÜÜTs.
---

Tags on original SHÜÜTs are selected manually during the final audio-review step. Do not infer or apply account tags from spoken handles or transcript text. A reSHÜÜT automatically tags the original publisher, whose identity is already known from the original post, and notifies them when the reSHÜÜT is first published.

**Why:** Spoken handles are too ambiguous and incorrect tags would notify the wrong person. The user chose a compact search field beneath the audio preview instead. The original publisher of a reSHÜÜT is unambiguous and the creator explicitly requested an automatic tag and notification for them, but later rejected the redundant “reSHÜÜT commentary / Publisher tagged” heading on published voice-note reSHÜÜTs.

**How to apply:** Keep the account picker optional and space-efficient on web and native for original SHÜÜTs. Confirm selected handles before publication. For reSHÜÜTs, show the publisher tag in the composer and notify that publisher once per new reSHÜÜT (not for edits or self-actions). Do not add the redundant tag/heading row above published voice commentary.
