---
name: Explicit post tagging
description: Product rule for tagging people in SHÜÜTs.
---

Tags are selected manually during the final audio-review step. Do not infer or apply account tags from spoken handles or transcript text.

**Why:** Spoken handles are too ambiguous and incorrect tags would notify the wrong person. The user chose a compact search field beneath the audio preview instead.

**How to apply:** Keep the account picker optional and space-efficient on web and native. Confirm selected handles before publication, and create notifications only after the SHÜÜT is published.
