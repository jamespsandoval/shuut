---
name: Launch content review
description: Product decision on automated pre-publication content review during SHÜÜT's early launch.
---

Do not reintroduce automated pre-publication content classification or an operator approval requirement for voice posts, comments, or reSHÜÜTs without the creator explicitly choosing it. Keep transcription readiness, account safety, blocks, deletion, and manual enforcement distinct from automatic content review.

**Why:** The creator said it was too early to moderate content and explicitly requested removal of content review. The unattended approval queue had kept otherwise transcription-ready posts out of the feed when no production operator was authorized.

**How to apply:** When changing publication, feed visibility, voice processing disclosures, or review tooling, do not make content wait on an AI classification result or an operator action. Reporting and blocking are separate safety capabilities, not pre-publication approval.
