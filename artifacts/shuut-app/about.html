About SHÜÜT

SHÜÜT is a voice-note-first social platform where people share short recordings alongside readable transcripts. Read a thought when you have a moment. Press play when you want to hear the person behind it.

What happens on SHÜÜT

Record a short voice note. SHÜÜT turns your words into a transcript, so others can read the post or listen to the original recording. Follow people, react to posts, reply with your voice and reSHÜÜT something you want to share with others.
